# Country/language coverage tracker — first-pass universe and progress

**Evidence checked:** 2026-10-24 UTC. This is a bounded, incremental research ledger, not an exhaustive host survey. The research universe is the UN’s 193 Member States plus 2 Observer States (195 rows); Taiwan is separately tracked. The coverage status says whether a logged localized query/source review occurred, not whether a provider exists or is available to a resident.

## Progress snapshot

- **Counts:** 178 searched_limited, 16 searched_focused_candidate, 0 partial/operator-only, 1 broad-global, 0 unsearched.
- **Strict candidate file:** 38 evidence-backed static/client-side services. Separate paid/restricted/unverified lead register: 195. See JSON for exact queries, languages, source URLs, claims and unresolved fields.
- **Suitability:** no provider is vetted/approved for GhostPay/P2P/payment/key-handling UI. Confirmed provider ads/branding, analytics/CDP/script insertion, terms and unknown served-asset integrity are blockers for key-critical use until independently resolved. “Unknown” is not green.
- **Regional discoveries:** new bounded passes added coverage for Latin America, MENA/Central Africa, Caribbean/islands/Pacific, and Europe; five additional localized candidates were added (UK Static Host, Czech Endora Free, TUKE institution-only, XServer Static in Japan, WebHostingKashmir in India). Many leads remain conditional, trial-only, paid, stale, unavailable or unresolved; details are separate. There are **no unsearched UN rows now**, but a limited query per country is not an exhaustive per-country audit.
- **Method status:** this first-pass configured web-search method did not expose its engine name. The independently requested second method/search-engine pass is **not started**. Blockchain/content-addressed provider catalog is also **not started**; protocol feasibility is separately discussed in architecture notes.

## Suitability and safety note

This report separates general art/docs/mirror sites from payment/key-handling apps. No provider in this research seed is approved or vetted for GhostPay/P2P/payment/key UI. A free host that injects ads or analytics/CDP scripts, or whose served-asset integrity is unknown, is unsuitable by default for key-handling/payment apps until explicit permission, no-injection/tracking, CSP/CORS, privacy/data-handling and safe-auth controls are verified. Artwork labels do not change the app’s actual payment function. See `mirror_suitability` and `hosted_asset_modification` on each provider record. Provider pages are first-party claims, not an independent uptime/security audit.
