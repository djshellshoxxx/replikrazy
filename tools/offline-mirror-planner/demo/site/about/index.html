About the notebook

Lantern Field Notes is a fictional community notebook. The walkers, the meadow and the river in these pages do not exist. The site was written to give the Offline Mirror Planner something realistic to inventory: nested folders, a stylesheet, a script, images, a text download and a few files that should never be mirrored.

House rules

  • No names. Entries are signed with a lantern colour, if at all.
  • No exact locations. "Near the old fence" is plenty.
  • Short is better than complete.

Questions about this demo go to notes@example.org.
