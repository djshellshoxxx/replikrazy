A shared notebook kept by an imaginary group of evening walkers. We carry small lanterns, write down what the light shows us, and keep the pages short. Everything on this site is invented for a software demo.

How the notebook works

  1. Walk a route you already know.
  2. Keep the lantern low and the notes lower.
  3. Write three lines: what you saw, what you heard, what you guessed.
