River survey, lower bend

Entry 2 · signed: green lantern

Mist sat on the water in a flat sheet about knee high. With the lantern held low the reeds threw long shadows across it, like the lines on a page.

Saw: one heron, standing very still, then not there.
Heard: water moving under the footbridge.
Guessed: the heron comes back to the same stone each night.
