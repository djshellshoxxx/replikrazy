First light on the east meadow

Entry 1 · signed: amber lantern

We started at the gate just after the sky went grey. The grass was wet enough to hold the lantern light in small beads, so the path looked like it had been drawn in dots.

Saw: two moths circling the lantern, a fence post with a ribbon tied to it.
Heard: a gate somewhere far off, opening and not closing.
Guessed: the ribbon marks the halfway point for someone else's walk.
